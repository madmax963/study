sub-DAG

Deep dive: Snowflake (the platform)

A cloud data platform — not just a schema, not just "another warehouse." It runs on top of AWS/GCP/Azure, so it complements the cross-cloud breadth in Stage 04 rather than competing with it. Learn it roughly in this order:

01 · Architecture ★
Three layers: cloud storage, separated compute (virtual warehouses), and cloud services — compute and storage scale independently.
The #1 conceptual interview question; explains Snowflake's pricing and performance model.
02 · Virtual warehouses ★
Sizing (XS→6XL), auto-suspend/auto-resume, multi-cluster warehouses for concurrency, scaling up vs out.
Directly controls cost and query speed — the lever a DE tunes daily.
03 · Micro-partitions & clustering
How Snowflake auto-partitions data, pruning, clustering keys, and when to set them.
The Snowflake equivalent of partitioning/indexing — key to query optimization.
04 · Loading & ingestion ★
COPY INTO, stages (internal/external), Snowpipe (continuous/serverless), Snowpipe Streaming.
The bread-and-butter ingestion path into Snowflake.
05 · Streams & Tasks ★
Streams (CDC/change tracking) + Tasks (scheduled SQL) to build native pipelines and incremental models.
Snowflake's built-in way to do CDC and orchestration without external tools.
06 · Time Travel & cloning
Querying past data (AT/BEFORE), undropping objects, instant zero-copy clones of tables/schemas/databases.
Almost-unique features — common interview talking points and real dev/test wins.
07 · Snowpark
Running Python/Java/Scala (DataFrames + UDFs) pushed down to Snowflake compute, instead of pulling data out.
The modern alternative to Spark for in-warehouse processing.
08 · Semi-structured data
The VARIANT type, FLATTEN, querying JSON/Avro/Parquet directly in SQL.
DEs constantly land semi-structured data; Snowflake handles it natively.
09 · Security & access
Role-based access control (RBAC), role hierarchies, masking policies, row-access policies.
Ties straight into the governance/security content in Stage 05.
10 · Sharing & Marketplace
Secure data sharing, reader accounts, and the Snowflake Marketplace.
The "data-as-a-product" angle — connects to data mesh in Stage 07.
11 · Cost management ★
Credit-based billing, resource monitors, warehouse right-sizing, query profiling.
FinOps is now a core DE responsibility; cost questions come up in every Snowflake interview.
The Spark Notes optimization mindset (pruning, partitioning, avoiding shuffles, cost) transfers directly — Snowflake just exposes those levers differently. And dbt is most commonly run on Snowflake, so learn the two together.

  1. Snowflake platform + dbt — the modern warehouse + transformation pairing, learn together
  2. Docker / Kubernetes — containerization
  3. Terraform & CI/CD — infrastructure and automation, treated properly, not in passing

  5. Cross-cloud breadth — de-GCP-ify your knowledge
